# Harvest glyph development evidence

`allyeodrum/*.png` come from local video `jfFi5GeGzUg`, reconstructed native
system 003 (source interval is in its scan.json). The source is retained at
`truth/harvest/validation/ally-template-source.png`. At native staff spacing
18.278 px, glyph centers were filled kick (94, pos 1), filled snare (162, pos 5),
cross (162, pos 9), circled cross (94, pos 9). Crops cover 1.6 staff spaces and
are normalized to 11 px spacing to match the existing template convention.
This system is excluded from the harvest head audit. The circled shape does
not establish its instrument or articulation: its channel mapping is unverified.
No glyph is evidence of acoustic timing or a physical kit key.
